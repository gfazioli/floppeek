Privacy policy

The short version: Floppeek has no account, no telemetry and no analytics, and it never uses the network. This website sets no cookies of its own.

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Who is responsible

The data controller is Giovambattista Fazioli (Undolog), an independent software developer in Italy, VAT number IT12343751009. Write to privacy@undolog.com about anything on this page. See also the Legal notice.

The app

  • The two extensions, the preview and the thumbnail, run in Apple's App Sandbox without the network entitlement. macOS hands them the one file being previewed; they read it and draw it, and keep nothing. macOS caches the thumbnails it shows, as it does for every file.
  • The app only explains what to do and opens System Settings when you ask. It sends nothing anywhere, and keeps no data of its own; macOS may remember its window's position, as for any app.
  • No telemetry, no analytics and no crash reports. Floppeek never updates itself: a new version comes from this site or from Homebrew.
  • Downloading Floppeek from GitHub, or installing it with Homebrew, goes through those services, whose own privacy policies apply.

This website

  • No cookies of its own, no analytics, no cross-site tracking, nothing kept in your browser's storage.
  • Hosting. The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub processes your IP address and the details of each request to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We have no access to those logs; GitHub keeps them under its own privacy statement.
  • Release information. The home page asks GitHub's API for Floppeek's releases, to link the download and show the release notes, so GitHub sees your IP address.
  • Sponsors' pictures are loaded from GitHub, which sees your IP address. The fonts are served by the site itself.
  • A link to GitHub Sponsors or to a donation through Stripe opens their page, under their own privacy policies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, and object to its use; for this app and site we hold none. You can also complain to the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
